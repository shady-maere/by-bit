Bitcoin & Cryptocurrency Exchange FAQs

Everything you need to know about buying, selling, trading and earning crypto on Bybit.

How do I buy Bitcoin on Bybit?
Creating an account takes under a minute. Sign up with your email or mobile number, verify your identity, then choose from multiple payment methods — bank card, bank transfer, P2P or a deposit in 65+ fiat currencies — to buy Bitcoin (BTC) instantly at market-leading prices with zero maker fees on spot.
Is Bybit a safe and legitimate cryptocurrency exchange?
Yes. Bybit uses bank-grade security including cold-wallet storage, multi-signature protection, mandatory two-factor authentication and 24/7 real-time risk monitoring. A dedicated Risk Control team operates around the clock, and a transparent Proof-of-Reserves is published so you can verify your funds are fully backed 1:1.
Which cryptocurrencies can I trade on Bybit?
Bybit supports 300+ digital assets including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), XRP, Dogecoin (DOGE), Cardano (ADA) and the MNT token — across spot, futures, options and TradFi perpetual markets, so you can build a diversified portfolio from one account.
How much does Bybit charge in trading fees?
Bybit offers some of the lowest fees in the industry. Spot trading starts at 0% maker fees for regular users, P2P trading is completely fee-free, and futures fees are tiered with discounts for high-volume traders. There are no hidden charges on deposits.
Does Bybit have a mobile app?
Yes — the Bybit app is available on iOS and Android and rated 4.7/5 by millions of users. You can buy crypto, trade spot and futures, earn yield, spend with the Bybit Card and manage your portfolio from anywhere, with the same security as the web platform.
Can I earn interest on my crypto without trading?
Absolutely. Bybit Earn lets you put idle funds to work with flexible and fixed-term products. New users can start with USDT at up to 20% APR, while BTC, ETH and USDC earn competitive flexible rates with no lock-up — and flexible products let you withdraw anytime.
